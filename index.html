How a repair works

Five steps, no surprises.

Every job follows the same order, so you always know where your car stands.

  1. Drop off

    Book online or call. Use the key drop box if you come before we open.

  2. We diagnose

    A technician inspects the problem and checks the basics while it's on the lift.

  3. You get a call

    We explain what we found and give you a written estimate by text or email.

  4. You decide

    Nothing gets fixed until you say yes. Want a second opinion? No hard feelings.

  5. Pick up

    We text when it's ready and walk you through what we did.
